# The Ash-Black Ballad of The Crown of Burned Names

Hear now, if your ears have not been bought,  
And keep your tongue beneath your breath;  
For I sing of a ward from the ruin-wrought,  
A circlet that bargains with memory and death.

**O The Crown of Burned Names, black in the flame,**  
**Guard what is living, unmake what is named;**  
**A hush for the lost, a veil for the known—**  
**Who wears it keeps watch, but is never alone.**

It was forged in **266 AS**, so the old scraps say,  
Though old scraps lie sweetly when ashes are deep.  
No bell gave witness, no herald cried,  
Only sparks in the dark and a vow denied.

Its **attunement cost is 60**—  
Mark that well, and mark it twice.  
Some pay with blood, some pay with breath,  
Some pay by forgetting the shape of death.

It is a crown of blackened material,  
Scarred with names that the fire could not spare;  
Each letter is wounded, each wound is a door,  
And the brow beneath it is less than before.

Its silhouette stands stark and funerary,  
A gallows of shadow, a chapel of bone.  
No jewel adorns it, no bright metal sings;  
The dark keeps its counsel around its rings.

**O The Crown of Burned Names, black in the flame,**  
**Guard what is living, unmake what is named;**  
**A hush for the lost, a veil for the known—**  
**Who wears it keeps watch, but is never alone.**

I have seen it hung where the cold lamps fail,  
And I have seen dust settle upon its rim.  
I say it was waiting; I say it was mourned—  
But dust has a voice when the mind is worn.

It brings no thunder, no radiant sign,  
No answering choir from under the stone.  
It merely stills the room around it,  
Till every small breath sounds borrowed and owned.

Remembrance enters with a covered face;  
Loss follows softly and will not depart.  
Guarded identity sits at the table,  
With one empty place and a sealed-up heart.

That is the tension the burned names bear:  
Protection in one hand, erasure in the other.  
The ward keeps watch over what must remain,  
While the fire takes the name from the brother.

**O The Crown of Burned Names, black in the flame,**  
**Guard what is living, unmake what is named;**  
**A hush for the lost, a veil for the known—**  
**Who wears it keeps watch, but is never alone.**

So pass it not lightly from palm into palm,  
Nor call it a prize for the bold or the brave.  
A crown may defend what the world would devour,  
Yet leave only silence to mark what it saves.

And if, in the dark, you hear someone sing  
A name that the blackened scars have concealed,  
Do not answer aloud. Let the lost remain lost.  
Some doors are protection; some doors are sealed.
