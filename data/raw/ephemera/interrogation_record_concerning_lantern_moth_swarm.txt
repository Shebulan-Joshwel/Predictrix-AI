# Ash-Black Interrogation Record: Lantern Moth Swarm, Cinder Shrike, Thorn Wraith

*[A shuttered chamber. One lamp burns upon the table. The Recorder speaks from behind the screen. The Interrogated Witness is not identified in this copy. At each question, the lamp-flame gutters, though no wind is admitted.]*

**INTERROGATOR:** State what you know of the first manifestation.

**WITNESS:** The Lantern Moth Swarm.

**INTERROGATOR:** Describe it without prayer, ornament, or the coward’s habit of calling every shadow an omen.

**WITNESS:** The Lantern Moth Swarm is a dense cloud of moths carrying the eerie visual impression of lantern-light. It does not move as separate vermin. It acts as a collective.

**INTERROGATOR:** Its purpose?

**WITNESS:** It is a siege-breaker of serious threat, bringing mothlike disruption against fortified positions.

**INTERROGATOR:** Can it be escaped?

**WITNESS:** It feels restless, overwhelming, and difficult to escape.

*[The witness rubs both eyes. The Recorder notes a pale dust upon the table, though no dust was present before the questioning. This may be an error of the lamp.]*

**INTERROGATOR:** Now the second. Name it.

**WITNESS:** Cinder Shrike.

**INTERROGATOR:** What makes it feared?

**WITNESS:** The Cinder Shrike is a threat of extreme severity, acting as a nocturnal ambusher. Its attacks are defined by surprise and darkness.

**INTERROGATOR:** Its shape?

**WITNESS:** A shrike-like silhouette marked by the blackened, ember-dusted aspect of cinder.

**INTERROGATOR:** Its manner?

**WITNESS:** Silent. Watchful. Violently sudden.

*[At the words “violently sudden,” the lamp goes out. There is no recorded sound of a door opening, footfall, or wingbeat. The lamp is relit. The witness is still present, unless the Recorder has mistaken the shape in the chair.]*

**INTERROGATOR:** The last one. Speak clearly.

**WITNESS:** Thorn Wraith.

**INTERROGATOR:** Is it flesh?

**WITNESS:** It belongs as much to the terror of sleep as to the waking world.

**INTERROGATOR:** That is evasion.

**WITNESS:** Thorn Wraith is a dream-feeder, drawing its nature from the invasion and consumption of dreams.

**INTERROGATOR:** Appearance.

**WITNESS:** A ghostly form threaded with thorn-like shapes, giving its outline a cruel and insubstantial character.

**INTERROGATOR:** Temper.

**WITNESS:** Intimate and predatory. It haunts the mind with the patience of hunger.

*[Silence follows. The Interrogator orders the witness to repeat the final name. The witness does not answer. From behind the screen, the Recorder hears scratching, though no writing hand is moving.]*

**RECORDER’S CLOSING NOTE:** The testimony is entered as given. Whether the witness described these three creatures, or merely gave them room to listen, cannot be established.
