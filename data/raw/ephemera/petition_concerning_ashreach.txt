# Petition for the Proper Accounting of Ashreach and Fenspire

**To the hands that receive petitions, and to all who judge a written plea:**

I, **Caedmon Fellgard**, a minor figure and **Scribe**, set down this petition in the disciplined manner required of written records. My office centers on the production and keeping of such records. Let no mark be mistaken for rumor, nor any omission taken as peace. I write with the reserved and meticulous care proper to a record-keeper, though the marsh air has stained the page and the ink runs like a dark wound.

**Concerning Ashreach:**

Ashreach lies in **The Weeping Marshes** and is currently contested. Its garrison numbers **2598**. Therefore let it be recorded as a defended but unsettled holding, rather than a core stronghold. Its aspect is bleak and marsh-bound, a settlement caught between ash and water. Its atmosphere is tense, unstable, and perpetually threatened.

For this reason, I petition that Ashreach not be counted among secure possessions merely because its walls bear arms. A garrison does not make stillness, and a defended place is not thereby settled. Let its contested condition remain plainly written in every ledger, proclamation, and military accounting, lest neat script conceal the danger beneath it.

**Concerning Fenspire:**

Fenspire stands in the **Gloaming Reach** and was founded in **57 AS**. It is a core location, with a garrison of **3,742**, and its status is garrisoned. Its silhouette is defined by a stark spire rising above the dim character of the Gloaming Reach. It projects disciplined vigilance; its atmosphere is shaped by permanent military occupation.

I petition that Fenspire be recorded according to this weight: not as a doubtful outpost, nor as a holding awaiting settlement, but as a core location whose vigilance is constant and whose garrisoned status is plain. Let the number **3,742** remain unabridged. Let **57 AS** remain beside its founding, so that neither neglect nor convenient memory may loosen its place in the record.

Thus I ask for no embellishment, only distinction: Ashreach marked as defended yet unsettled and currently contested; Fenspire marked as core, garrisoned, and permanently watchful. May the ledgers preserve what the living would rather blur.

**Entered and kept by my hand,**

**Caedmon Fellgard**  
**Scribe**
