# Interrogation Record: Sabelle Blackspire Concerning the Salt-Blind Leviathan

**[Record opened. Lamp oil low. The questioning chamber is otherwise unmarked.]**

**RECORDER:** State your name and office for the record.

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** Sabelle Blackspire. Quartermaster.

**RECORDER:** State your birth year.

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** 341 AS.

**RECORDER:** You are entered here as a minor figure of the Ashen Era. Do you contest the entry?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** I contest nothing entered by a hand that holds the seal.

**[The recorder pauses. No physical description is entered for Sabelle Blackspire. Her visual identity, in this record, is limited to her role as a Quartermaster. No canonical temperament is established; the transcript supplies no conclusion beyond her spoken answers.]**

**RECORDER:** We will speak of Salt-Blind Leviathan.

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** Then speak.

**RECORDER:** Is Salt-Blind Leviathan a beast, an engine, or a weapon?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** A creature.

**RECORDER:** A creature capable of breaking a siege?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** It is a siege-breaker.

**RECORDER:** Of what threat?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** Moderate threat.

**RECORDER:** You call that moderate?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** I repeat the measure.

**[The recorder notes no tremor in the lamp flame and no alteration in the subject’s recorded posture. The absence of such signs proves nothing.]**

**RECORDER:** Describe it.

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** Vast. Pale. Salt-crusted.

**RECORDER:** Its eyes?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** Sightless.

**RECORDER:** Then how does it find a wall? A gate? A living body?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** It does not rely on sight.

**RECORDER:** On what, then?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** Pressure.

**RECORDER:** Explain.

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** It comes on. That is explanation enough.

**RECORDER:** You claim immense destructive force?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** I claim what is visible.

**RECORDER:** And its manner?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** Ponderous. Relentless.

**RECORDER:** Does it halt when challenged?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** No answer entered.

**RECORDER:** Does it distinguish soldier from stone?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** No answer entered.

**RECORDER:** Did you command Salt-Blind Leviathan?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** I am a Quartermaster.

**RECORDER:** That is not an answer.

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** It is the answer I have.

**[A scrape is heard. The nature of the sound is not determined. The recorder’s ink blots across the line beneath the subject’s name.]**

**RECORDER:** One final time. What should be written?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** Write the name correctly.

**RECORDER:** Salt-Blind Leviathan.

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** Write that it cannot see.

**RECORDER:** And yet it advances?

**SABELLE BLACKSPIRE:** Write that too.

**[Record closed. No confession, denial, or further statement is entered.]**
