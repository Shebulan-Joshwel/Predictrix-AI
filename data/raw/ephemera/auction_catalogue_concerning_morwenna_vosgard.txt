# Catalogue of the Unseen Sapper: Morwenna Vosgard

**Being a modest offering of records, impressions, and empty places concerning Morwenna Vosgard, minor character of the Ashen Era**

Let the buyer be warned: this catalogue contains no portrait. No physical features are recorded for **Morwenna Vosgard**. Her visual identity is associated with the work of a **Sapper**, and no faithful likeness may therefore be promised beneath any hood, mask, or veil.

The five powers of the realm remain named upon the margins of this sale: **the Ashen Vanguard, House Morvain, the Silent Choir, the Iron-Ring Cartel, and the Bleeding Crown**. Whether any would claim this material is not recorded. Whether any would deny it is likewise not recorded.

---

### Lot A — Birth Entry, 296 AS

A plain leaf bearing the single useful certainty of this catalogue:

> **Morwenna Vosgard was born in 296 AS.**

The hand is cramped, the ink faded, and the surrounding entries offer no clarification. This is not offered as proof of lineage, station, or allegiance. It establishes only the year given above.

---

### Lot B — The Sapper’s Mark

A collection of indistinct impressions: scored earth, broken stone, a darkened tool-head, and the suggestion of labor performed where labor is least praised.

These images are not portraits. They are offered because Morwenna Vosgard’s visual identity is associated with the work of a Sapper. The catalogue makes no claim that these objects were hers, that she touched them, or that they were present at any particular undertaking.

The discerning bidder will observe that absence may be the most honest likeness available.

---

### Lot C — Service Description

A narrow strip of vellum, undated and without seal, reading:

> **Morwenna Vosgard serves as a Sapper.**

No rank is supplied. No employer is supplied. No account of her duties is supplied beyond the title of her service. Buyers are urged to resist the common hunger to make a life from a single occupation.

---

### Lot D — Temperament, Unentered

A blank folio, carefully preserved.

No temperament is recorded for Morwenna Vosgard. The folio contains neither praise nor accusation, neither tenderness nor spite. It is not evidence of gentleness, cruelty, courage, fear, obedience, or defiance. It is simply the place where such testimony fails to appear.

---

### Lot E — The Absent Portrait

An empty frame, offered without image.

This final lot gathers the catalogue’s several deficiencies: no physical features recorded, no temperament recorded, and no further particulars that may safely be declared. It is presented for those who understand that a minor character may still cast a long shadow—though this catalogue cannot say where that shadow falls.

**Bidding shall proceed in silence.**
