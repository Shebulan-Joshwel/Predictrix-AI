# **The Ashen Hammer’s Catalogue of Quarantined Rarities**

*Issued for the consideration of bidders of sound purse and doubtful conscience. The house accepts no blame for chills, hauntings, censures, or the sudden remembering of sins.*

---

## **Thorncairn — Place of Withheld Passage**

There are roads that invite the traveller, and there are roads that merely permit him to die upon them. Thorncairn belongs to the latter kind.

This stronghold—or ruin, or both, according to which mouth speaks—stands in the Gloaming Reach. It was founded in **192 AS**, when the world was already old with ash and treachery. Thorncairn is a **core location**, though such distinctions matter little to those barred from its gates. Its present status is **quarantined**.

The appearance of Thorncairn is defined by thorned, cairn-like austerity against the dimness of the Gloaming Reach: a place of harsh accumulation, black angles, and stone that seems to have been piled by grief rather than masons. It withdraws from the eye. It offers no welcome. Even its silence feels posted with a warning.

The quarantine lends Thorncairn an atmosphere of dread and exclusion, which is perhaps the only honest hospitality left in the Ashen Era. No claim is made here that passage is possible, safe, or wise. The catalogue merely records that Thorncairn exists, and that the bold may bid for the privilege of contemplating its name.

**Condition:** Forbidding. Withdrawn. Unwelcoming to the living and, one suspects, to certain categories of the dead.

## **Hesper Wrenfield — Outrider, Minor Figure of the Ashen Era**

Hesper Wrenfield was born in **404 AS** and serves as an **Outrider**.

That is the full measure of certainty fit for public sale.

No canonical physical features are established for Hesper Wrenfield. The buyer is therefore warned against embellished portraits, counterfeit likenesses, and any seller who claims to recognize Hesper by scar, eye, gait, or other convenient invention. Their visual identity is limited to their role as an Outrider.

Likewise, no canonical temperament is established. Whether Hesper Wrenfield is dutiful, faithless, brave, exhausted, or merely difficult to kill remains outside the record. The house advises bidders to distrust anyone who fills that silence too confidently.

**Condition:** Recorded, but not explained. A minor figure, which is often how history labels those who survive it.

## **Gauntlet of Sorrowfell — Regalia of Grim Authority**

A piece of regalia bearing the name **Gauntlet of Sorrowfell**.

Its appearance is that of regalia bearing the name Gauntlet of Sorrowfell: ceremonial, severe, and made ominous by the sorrow gathered in its title. It carries an atmosphere of sorrow and grim authority, as though command itself had been forced to kneel before mourning.

The **attunement cost is 12**.

Its forging date is **not recorded in the supplied facts**. Any claim otherwise should be treated as a lie dressed for court.

**Condition:** Regalia. Sorrow-laden. Authoritative enough to make an honest hand tremble.

*Bidding shall proceed for each entry separately, and the house shall keep its silence where silence is the only surviving form of provenance.*
