### Marginal Memorandum

Crookvale is rebuilt, but not softened. Its crooked identity suits the highland air, the hard edges of its structure, and the manner in which it endures outside the core holdings. The garrison stands at **1004**, which is a number large enough to defend a place and small enough to be consumed by one bad accounting. Thus the count must remain visible.

The **Gauntlet of Sorrowfell** is entered on the same sheet only for orderly reference. A shared page is not a shared history. Its forging date is not recorded. Its attunement cost is **12**. Its presence in the register proves only that the name has been preserved for inspection.

Let the next hand keep the columns clean. Crookvale has survived disorder. The ledger need not imitate it.
