# Catalogue of Ash-Waste Curiosities, Sealed Records, and Wardenly Testimony

*Issued under ash-grey seal; to be read by lamplight and believed only where the ink survives.*

## First Offering — The Stormmarch Dossier

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Subject | **Stormmarch** |
| Provenance | A fortified settlement lying in the Ash Wastes, founded in **71 AS** |
| Present condition | Its garrison numbers **9,263** |
| Restriction | The stronghold remains quarantined and outside the realm’s core holdings |
| Character | Sealed away and severe; military strength held beneath quarantine |
| Offered matter | A survey of walls, gate customs, ashfall precautions, and the legal silence surrounding entry |

Stormmarch is not a pleasant holding, nor is it offered as one. Its visual identity is that of a fortified settlement shaped by the harsh, ash-laden character of the Ash Wastes. Those who prize walls may admire it. Those who prize breathing may refrain.

The dossier contains no promise that the quarantine shall lift. It contains only the fact of the quarantine, which is often dearer to desperate buyers than any promise.

## Second Offering — The Herbalist’s Register

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Subject | **Ignatz Fellgard** |
| Calling | Herbalist |
| Standing | A minor figure of the Ashen Era |
| Birth record | Born in **257 AS** |
| Portraiture | No canonical physical traits or distinctive visual features are established |
| Temperament | No canonical temperament or emotional disposition is established |

This lot is a narrow register of an unadorned life: remedies, preparations, and the quiet authority sometimes granted to those who know what grows beneath ash. Let no bidder mistake the lack of a portrait for evidence of plainness, ugliness, or beauty. The record does not say. Let no buyer attach kindness, cruelty, fear, or courage to Ignatz Fellgard. The record does not say that either.

A prudent catalogue leaves blank spaces where gossip would place a face.

| Bearing | Guarded solemnity, sharpened by the secrecy of a keeper who sells intelligence |

No physical features of Aldous Wrenfield the Last Warden are established. What is offered is not a likeness, but the weight of custody, secrecy, and compromised watchfulness.

Bids shall be written, folded, and surrendered without witness. The auctioneer accepts no questions concerning what was kept, what was sold, or who first opened the seal.
