# Compact of Sapper-Service Concerning Ederon Coldwater

Let this instrument be read before witnesses, sealed where a seal may hold, and entered upon the ash-ledger without embellishment.

Concerning **Ederon Coldwater**, this compact records the following truth:

**Ederon Coldwater is a minor figure of the Ashen Era, born in 391 AS. He serves as a Sapper.**

No other rank, title, allegiance, inheritance, or distinction shall be attached to his name by this writing. The office receiving this compact may command only such labor as belongs properly to a Sapper: the handling of earth, stone, ruin, buried hazard, breach, obstruction, and other works placed before him under that service. Where the ground is false, the wall is hollow, or the dust conceals a peril, Ederon Coldwater shall give warning to the authority present, insofar as warning may be given without abandoning the assigned work.

No promise is made herein concerning victory, survival, reward, advancement, or remembrance. The ash keeps poor accounts, and those who seek certainty beneath broken ground often find only their own bones.

The contracting office shall not declare Ederon Coldwater brave, fearful, loyal, faithless, gentle, cruel, swift, dull, willing, or unwilling unless such judgment is separately witnessed and lawfully entered. **No canonical temperament is established for Ederon Coldwater.** His demeanor shall therefore not be supplied by rumor, portrait, barrack-talk, or the convenient invention of a clerk.

Likewise, no likeness shall be demanded of this record. **No canonical physical features are established for Ederon Coldwater; his visual identity is limited to his role as a Sapper.** Any sketch, carving, mask, or painted sign claiming to show his face shall be treated as an unverified addition and not as part of this compact.

The bearer of this name remains answerable for the tasks properly set before him, but this instrument shall not transform a working office into a lineage, a legend, or a greater station than the record permits. Let the service be weighed as service, and the man be named as named.

Should any clause herein be challenged, the plain entries concerning his birth, his standing, and his occupation shall take precedence over ornament, hearsay, and favorable invention. The rest may be struck away without harming the truth of the record.

Thus is **Ederon Coldwater** entered: born in **391 AS**, a **minor figure of the Ashen Era**, serving as a **Sapper**.

*Seal, mark, or witness left to the authority holding this compact.*
