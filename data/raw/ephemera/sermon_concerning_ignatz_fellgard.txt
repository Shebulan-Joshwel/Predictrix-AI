# **Sermon of the Unlifted Hand: Concerning Ignatz Fellgard and the Gauntlet of Sorrowfell**

Hear me, ash-breathed faithful, and keep your hands beneath your sleeves.

There are names that thunder from ruined towers, and names that pass quietly between the sickbed and the herb-basket. **Ignatz Fellgard** is of the latter kind. He is not set among the great captains, nor counted as a crowned judge, nor praised as a maker of splendid ruin. The record gives him one service plainly:

**Ignatz Fellgard — Herbalist.**

Do not despise the small office. A leaf may be small and still bitter enough to teach the tongue. A root may lie unseen beneath the ash and yet hold a fever down. Whether Ignatz Fellgard heals, merely prepares, or only knows what the green things conceal, I cannot swear beyond the record. But the record says **Herbalist**, and a true sermon must not make a larger man merely because its speaker desires a larger lesson.

The ledger also says:

**Ignatz Fellgard was born in 257 AS.**

Mark the year. **257 AS.** Let no careless mouth replace it with a prettier number, for the dead and the living are both poorly served by ornamental lies.

Now consider the **Gauntlet of Sorrowfell**. It is regalia, bearing the name **Gauntlet of Sorrowfell**, and around it clings an atmosphere of sorrow and grim authority. Its forging date is not recorded. Thus we shall not invent one, though the ignorant always prefer a false date to an honest blank.

Its attunement cost is **12**.

Twelve. Not eleven, not thirteen. Twelve is the measure demanded before the gauntlet yields itself to a bearer. Yet understand this: I do not proclaim that Ignatz Fellgard wears it. I do not proclaim that he forged it, found it, commands it, or even stood within sight of it. Such claims are smoke, and smoke has no testimony.

Nor shall I give Ignatz Fellgard a false face. No canonical physical traits or distinctive visual features are established for Ignatz Fellgard. Nor shall I hang a false heart upon him. No canonical temperament or emotional disposition is established for Ignatz Fellgard. He is not made clearer by invention. He is made clearer by restraint.

So learn the narrow mercy of the ash: name what is known, bow before what is not, and fear the hand that reaches for authority without paying its due.

The gauntlet asks **12**.

The record asks truth.

And the sorrowful hand, once lifted, may never again be mistaken for an empty one.
