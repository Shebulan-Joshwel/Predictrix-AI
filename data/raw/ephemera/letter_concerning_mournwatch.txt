# Letter Under Grey Seal Concerning Mournwatch

_To the one who keeps the eastern records,_

I write concerning Mournwatch, though I confess that the place is not easily held in ink. A settlement may be marked upon a map, measured by its walls, and entered into a ledger with a neat hand. Mournwatch resists such tidiness. It lies in the Gloaming Reach, beneath that dim and lingering character which makes every hour seem borrowed from the night.

Do not mistake its silence for peace.

Mournwatch is garrisoned. The fact is plain enough, and any fool who doubts it need only look upon the watchfires, the barred approaches, and the sentries who turn their faces toward every road before they turn them toward a speaker. The garrison does not parade. It waits. Even the changing of the watch is performed with the grave restraint of a burial rite.

Nor should you count it among the realm’s favored heartlands. Mournwatch stands outside the realm’s core holdings, and those who govern from nearer and warmer seats are pleased to remember this whenever provisions, coin, or relief are discussed. Yet they remember the place readily enough when danger is spoken of. Then Mournwatch becomes a shield, a warning-post, a necessary hardship borne by others.

Its appearance is that of a guarded settlement set beneath the dim character of the Gloaming Reach. The walls seem less built than braced against some approaching weight. Doors are shut early. Voices are kept low. A traveler arriving at dusk sees little welcome, but many eyes. I have heard it said that the stones themselves watch the road. I will not swear to that. I will say only that no one there behaves as though the stones are blind.

The demeanor of Mournwatch is somber and vigilant. Those words are repeated in reports because they are accurate, though they fail to convey the burden of them. There is no careless laughter in the garrison square, no idle leaning upon a spear, no confidence that tomorrow will resemble today. Men and women stand as though awaiting an alarm that has merely been delayed.

If the realm still possesses a core, Mournwatch is not within it. Perhaps that is why it sees more clearly what gathers beyond the light. Keep its name in the records, and do not soften the entry. Mournwatch lies in the Gloaming Reach. It is garrisoned. It is outside the realm’s core holdings. These are not ornaments of description, but warnings.

_Sealed by a hand that has no wish to be remembered_
