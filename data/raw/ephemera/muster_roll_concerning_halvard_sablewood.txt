## Supplemental Muster Notes

**Halvard Sablewood** is entered as a minor figure of the Ashen Era and serves as a scribe. This roll cannot swear to the shape of his face, the color of his raiment, the cast of his eyes, or any other bodily mark, for no canonical physical details are recorded. Those who seek him by sight may therefore seek in vain. Seek instead the work of a scribe: the ordered line, the corrected margin, the quiet burden of names preserved when greater men would rather be forgotten.

His demeanor is unrecorded. Any report calling him meek, bitter, loyal, fearful, or bold exceeds the warrant of this roll. The clerk has heard pages speak more plainly than men, though pages may also conceal a cut made before the ink dried.

**Aldous Wrenfield the Last Warden** is a major Reliquary Keeper, born in **315 AS**. No canonical physical features are established. The record gives only the custodial identity of a Reliquary Keeper and the epithet **“the Last Warden”** as his visual presence. Let the ignorant imagine armor, scars, keys, or a hood; this roll confirms none of them.

What it does confirm is grave: Aldous secretly sells intelligence to the **Iron-Ring Cartel**. His guarded solemnity is not empty ceremony. It is sharpened by the secrecy of a keeper who sells intelligence, and thus every silence around him may be mistaken for devotion, caution, or guilt. This office records the fact and leaves the judgment to harsher hands.

*No further service, allegiance, appearance, temperament, or connection is entered for either name.*
