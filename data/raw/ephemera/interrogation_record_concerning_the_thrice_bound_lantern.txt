# Interrogation Record: Concerning The Thrice-Bound Lantern

*[The record begins upon a stained sheet. The chamber is not identified. A hooded witness sits opposite the interrogator. Between them rests a covered object. The clerk’s hand trembles, though no tremor is admitted in the margin.]*

**INTERROGATOR:** State what you know of The Thrice-Bound Lantern.

**WITNESS:** I know the name was spoken before the cloth was drawn. I know the room grew quieter afterward.

**INTERROGATOR:** That is not an answer.

**WITNESS:** It is the only answer I trust.

*[The interrogator removes the covering. The witness leans away. The object is a lantern marked by three visible bindings. Its silhouette is severe, warding, without ornament that I could see. The flame within is not bright. It does not flicker, though the air moves.]*

**INTERROGATOR:** Look upon it.

**WITNESS:** I have looked.

**INTERROGATOR:** Then say what it is.

**WITNESS:** A restraint.

**INTERROGATOR:** A lantern.

**WITNESS:** You asked what it is.

**INTERROGATOR:** Who carried it here?

*[The witness turns toward the blank wall. No name is given. The interrogator strikes the table once. The sound is swallowed quickly, as if the chamber itself objected.]*

**WITNESS:** I did not see a carrier.

**INTERROGATOR:** Who forged it?

**WITNESS:** The record says The Thrice-Bound Lantern was a ward forged in 72 AS.

**INTERROGATOR:** And what does the record say its purpose was?

**WITNESS:** Its threefold binding defines it as a protective object. Its strength lies in restraint and containment.

**INTERROGATOR:** Whose danger does it contain?

**WITNESS:** Danger does not require a surname.

**INTERROGATOR:** You speak as though it listens.

**WITNESS:** It listens as a sealed door listens.

*[A pause. The lantern radiates guarded stillness. The witness’s eyes remain fixed upon the bindings, never upon the light.]*

**INTERROGATOR:** Is it safe?

**WITNESS:** Safe is a word used by those standing outside the ward.

**INTERROGATOR:** Is it dangerous?

**WITNESS:** It is protective.

**INTERROGATOR:** You evade.

**WITNESS:** I contain.

*[The interrogator reaches toward the lantern. The witness rises so violently that the chair falls backward. The flame narrows. The clerk records that the object seemed perpetually to be holding danger at bay, though the clerk admits no danger was visible.]*

**INTERROGATOR:** Sit down.

**WITNESS:** Cover it.

**INTERROGATOR:** Why?

**WITNESS:** Because three bindings are visible, and that means three warnings have already been given.

*[No further questions are entered. The covering is replaced. Whether the witness was released, removed, or remained in the chamber cannot be established from this record.]*
