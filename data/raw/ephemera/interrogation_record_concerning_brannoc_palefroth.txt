# Interrogation Record: The Hollow Edge at Mournwatch

**Place:** Mournwatch, a garrisoned settlement in the Gloaming Reach, outside the realm’s core holdings.  
**Subject:** Brannoc Palefroth, Falconer.  
**Object under examination:** The Hollow Edge, a ward forged in 216 AS.  
**Questioning authority:** Cerys Sablewood the Ashen, Master of Coin.

*[The chamber remains unlit save for the grey leaking through the arrow-slit. The Hollow Edge rests upon the table. It bears its name plainly, though the letters seem to withdraw from the eye. Its presence makes the room feel emptied of warmth and severity.]*

**Cerys Sablewood the Ashen:** State your name and office.

**Brannoc Palefroth:** Brannoc Palefroth. Falconer.

**Cerys:** Your birth?

**Brannoc:** 233 AS.

**Cerys:** Your death?

*[The subject does not answer at once. The ward gives no light.]*

**Brannoc:** 271 AS. During The Winter Reckoning.

**Cerys:** You handled The Hollow Edge?

**Brannoc:** I bore what was placed in my keeping. I did not make it. It was forged in 216 AS. Attunement to it costs 21.

**Cerys:** What does it guard?

**Brannoc:** An absence.

**Cerys:** That is not an answer.

**Brannoc:** It is the only answer the ward permits. The Hollow Edge is a ward of emptiness. It severs certainty first. A hand reaches for its hilt and forgets whether it reached. A vow is spoken and leaves no wound in the air.

**Cerys:** Did you carry it beyond Mournwatch?

**Brannoc:** Mournwatch stands where the Gloaming Reach keeps watch over itself. It is garrisoned, and it lies outside the realm’s core holdings. Such places are built to deny passage. The ward was not.

**Cerys:** When was Mournwatch founded?

**Brannoc:** According to the enemy's court records, Mournwatch was founded in 244 AS.

**Cerys:** Who commanded you?

*[No temperament is recorded for Brannoc Palefroth. No physical features are recorded either; the visual identity preserved in this record is only that of a Falconer’s work.]*

**Brannoc:** Questions are hooks. I have no bird for yours.

**Cerys:** You mistake restraint for ignorance.

*[Cerys Sablewood the Ashen maintains a controlled, calculating manner. No physical features are established for Cerys; “the Ashen” is the defining mark permitted to the record.]*

**Cerys:** Tell me what The Hollow Edge desires.

**Brannoc:** Wards do not desire. People do. The Hollow Edge merely makes the wanting hollow.

**Cerys:** And what do I desire?

**Brannoc:** Coin remembers every debt. Blood remembers every crown.

*[At this, the questioning is suspended. The object remains upon the table. No confession is entered.]*
