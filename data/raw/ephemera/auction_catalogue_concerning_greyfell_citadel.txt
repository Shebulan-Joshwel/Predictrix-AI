# **The Greyfell Catalogue of Abandoned Stone**
### *An Auction of Salvage, Relics, and Unclaimed Remnants from Greyfell Citadel*

> **To all creditors, collectors, oath-breakers, and persons of sufficient nerve:**  
> The following catalogue concerns the remains of Greyfell Citadel, founded in **62 AS** within the **Shattered Vale**. The Citadel is abandoned and lies outside the realm’s core holdings. No warranty is offered regarding sanctity, safety, ownership, or the continued good sense of any purchaser.

Greyfell is not presented as a handsome ruin. Its visual identity is one of grey stone and grey-hued collapse, rising from the Shattered Vale like a tooth left in a dead jaw. Whatever banners once hung there have been taken by weather or hand. Whatever garrison once watched the approaches has gone. The place retains the demeanor of an old desolation, its former stronghold status reduced to abandonment.

The auctioneer records that each offering has been removed from the Citadel or its immediate fallen works. Buyers shall judge the truth of this claim according to their own consciences.

---

## **Catalogue of Offerings**

### **The Gatehouse Ironwork**
Bent hinges, corroded fastenings, and a length of darkened bar. Useful to those who desire the appearance of old authority without the burden of maintaining a gate.

### **The Grey Chapel Stone**
A dressed block bearing shallow marks too worn for confident reading. It has been cleaned only of loose dirt. The purchaser is advised not to ask what prayers were once spoken over it.

### **The Watch-Seat**
A broken stone chair from an upper passage, with one arm intact. It is uncomfortable, cold, and entirely suited to the ruling temperament.

### **The Ashen Archive Board**
A warped board with traces of pegs and cord. No papers accompany it. Its former purpose is unknown, which makes it desirable to those who prefer mystery to evidence.

### **The Cistern Chain**
A length of iron chain, heavily scaled, offered as found. Whether it served water, prisoners, or some uglier need cannot be established.

### **The Valeward Shards**
Fragments of grey masonry gathered from a collapsed outer wall. They are suitable for memorials, thresholds, grave-markers, or the construction of another place destined to be abandoned.

### **The Unclaimed Standard-Rod**
A splintered staff with no surviving device. The wood is darkened, the metal collar pitted. It bears no readable allegiance.

### **The Citadel Dust**
Sealed in a plain vessel. The catalogue clerk insists it is masonry dust. The clerk has not opened the vessel.

---

All sales are final. No purchaser may claim that Greyfell Citadel was misrepresented: it is a ruin of grey stone in the Shattered Vale, founded in **62 AS**, abandoned, and outside the realm’s core holdings. Its silence is included without additional charge.
