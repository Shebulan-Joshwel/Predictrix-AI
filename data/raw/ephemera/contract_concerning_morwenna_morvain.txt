# **Contract of Gaoler’s Custody Concerning Marrowwatch**

Let this instrument be read beneath an ash-dark sky, and let no hand alter its words without witness.

Morwenna Morvain, who serves as a Gaoler, is hereby named in this contract as the **Gaoler**. Any person who receives, delivers, or claims custody under this instrument shall be called the **Claimant**, whether such person comes by writ, seal, command, ransom, or supplication.

The Gaoler shall acknowledge the Claimant’s custody only when the Claimant is presented before her, or when a written charge bearing lawful authority is placed into her keeping. The Gaoler shall not be required to swear that any prisoner is innocent, guilty, living, dead, whole, or sound. Her duty is custody, and custody alone, until a proper release is shown.

This contract concerns **Marrowwatch**. Marrowwatch was founded in **280 AS** in **The Ash Wastes**. It is now abandoned, its name standing as the remnant of a watch-post without a living garrison recorded. It appears as an empty, marrow-pale watchhold set against the ash wastes. The place is silent, desolate, and defined by neglect.

Therefore, no Claimant shall construe this instrument as proof that Marrowwatch is occupied, defended, provisioned, or held by any living garrison. No hearth, chain, gate, cell, or wall shall be presumed sound merely because its name is written here. The Gaoler may refuse any demand requiring her to treat the watchhold as inhabited when the watchhold is abandoned.

Morwenna Morvain was born in **258 AS**. No canonical physical traits or distinctive visual features are established for Morwenna Morvain. No canonical temperament or emotional disposition is established for Morwenna Morvain. Accordingly, no description of her face, body, manner, temper, mercy, cruelty, or fear shall be admitted as a term of this contract unless she herself supplies it beneath her mark.

The Claimant accepts that ash may cover the road, silence may answer the summons, and neglect may have entered every chamber of Marrowwatch. The Claimant further accepts that the Gaoler’s office does not make the abandoned watchhold living, nor does the watchhold’s name make the Gaoler its garrison.

Any dispute shall remain attached to this page until settled by release, counterseal, or the Gaoler’s written refusal. A spoken promise made beyond the sight of this contract shall carry no weight against its clauses.
