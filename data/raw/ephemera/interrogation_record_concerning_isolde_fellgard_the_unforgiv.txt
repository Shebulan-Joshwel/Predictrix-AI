# Interrogation Record: Gloammarch Ash-Gate

*Filed beneath the iron seal of the Gloammarch garrison. The chamber was ash-dark, and the lamps were kept low. Present: Isolde Fellgard the Unforgiven, Caedmon Hollowmere, and the unnamed recorder.*

---

**RECORDER:** State your name and station.

**ISOLDE FELLGARD THE UNFORGIVEN:** Isolde Fellgard the Unforgiven. Blood-Mage.

**RECORDER:** Your birth?

**ISOLDE:** 286 AS.

*[A pause. The prisoner’s hands remain still upon the table. The atmosphere around them is one of grievance held tightly, ambition kept beneath the tongue, and betrayal concealed behind a controlled face.]*

**RECORDER:** You are accused of plotting the death of your own liege.

**ISOLDE:** Accused by whom?

**RECORDER:** The record does not name the accuser.

**ISOLDE:** Then the record is a coward.

**CAEDMON HOLLOWMERE:** The Choir does not require a named tongue when the blood speaks plainly.

**ISOLDE:** And you speak for the Choir?

**CAEDMON:** I am Caedmon Hollowmere. Choir Novice.

**RECORDER:** Your birth?

**CAEDMON:** 314 AS.

*[No physical feature is entered for Caedmon Hollowmere. His identity in this record is limited to his role as a Choir Novice. No temperament is established; the recorder therefore declines to describe his manner beyond his spoken words.]*

**ISOLDE:** A novice hears what he is taught to hear.

**CAEDMON:** A Blood-Mage knows what blood remembers.

**RECORDER:** Isolde Fellgard the Unforgiven, did you plot the death of your own liege?

**ISOLDE:** “Own” is a word used by those who mistake obedience for law.

**RECORDER:** That is not an answer.

**ISOLDE:** It is the answer beneath the answer.

*[The prisoner smiles. The recorder notes no confession. The dangerous atmosphere does not lessen.]*

**CAEDMON:** You deny nothing.

**ISOLDE:** I deny your certainty.

**RECORDER:** This questioning occurs in Gloammarch, which stands in the Ash Wastes. Gloammarch was founded in 321 AS. Its garrison numbers 2,148. Its status remains contested.

**ISOLDE:** Then your walls are no proof of authority.

**RECORDER:** The walls are not under examination.

**ISOLDE:** Everything in Gloammarch is under examination. Every gate, oath, corpse, and banner. A place holding its ground amid persistent dispute cannot pretend its judgments are clean.

*[Outside, the garrison gives a muted change of watch. No banner is identified.]*

**CAEDMON:** The Silent Choir will hear the omission.

**ISOLDE:** Let it hear this: a liege who fears a Blood-Mage has already offered up their throat.

**RECORDER:** Is that a confession?

**ISOLDE:** No. It is instruction.

*Record suspended. No verdict entered.*
