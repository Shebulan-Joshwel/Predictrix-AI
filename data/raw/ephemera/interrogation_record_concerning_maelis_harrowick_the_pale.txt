# Interrogation Record: Maelis Harrowick the Pale and Ignatz Cindervale

*The following exchange was set down by a clerk whose hand was judged steady enough for court use. The place of questioning is omitted. The voices are marked only as **QUESTIONER**, **MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE**, and **IGNATZ CINDERVALE**.*

**[Silence. A chair scrapes across stone.]**

**QUESTIONER:** State your name and station.

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** Maelis Harrowick the Pale. Court Chronicler.

**QUESTIONER:** Major or minor?

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** Major, by the register.

**QUESTIONER:** Your birth?

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** 340 AS.

**QUESTIONER:** And the charge?

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** You have written it already.

**QUESTIONER:** Then answer it aloud. Did you plot the death of your own liege?

**[A pause long enough for the clerk to sand the first answer.]**

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** I plotted what the record required.

**QUESTIONER:** That is not an answer.

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** It is the only answer a chronicler can give without making himself the subject.

**QUESTIONER:** You admit the plot?

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** I admit that ink remembers what mouths deny.

**[The questioner turns from Maelis.]**

**QUESTIONER:** State your name and calling.

**IGNATZ CINDERVALE:** Ignatz Cindervale. Falconer.

**QUESTIONER:** Your standing?

**IGNATZ CINDERVALE:** Minor figure, if the Ashen Era’s rolls are to be trusted.

**QUESTIONER:** Your birth?

**IGNATZ CINDERVALE:** 274 AS.

**QUESTIONER:** Were you present when Maelis Harrowick the Pale plotted the death of their own liege?

**IGNATZ CINDERVALE:** I was present for no such thing.

**QUESTIONER:** Were you present anywhere near the matter?

**IGNATZ CINDERVALE:** Nearness is a word for hawks and towers. I tend birds. I do not tend plots.

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** He is being precise.

**QUESTIONER:** You know the Falconer?

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** I know the name entered before me.

**IGNATZ CINDERVALE:** I know the chronicler’s station. That is not the same as knowing the person.

**[The clerk notes that Maelis remains restrained and watchful. The clerk notes no such disposition for Ignatz Cindervale, and claims no authority to assign one.]**

**QUESTIONER:** Describe Maelis Harrowick the Pale.

**IGNATZ CINDERVALE:** I cannot.

**QUESTIONER:** Why?

**IGNATZ CINDERVALE:** No physical traits or distinctive visual features have been established for Ignatz Cindervale, and I will not establish any for another by guesswork.

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** Nor have any canonical physical features been established for me. “The Pale” is an epithet, not a portrait.

**QUESTIONER:** Then what remains?

**MAELIS HARROWICK THE PALE:** The station. The birth year. The treason.

**[The record ends beneath a blot.]**
