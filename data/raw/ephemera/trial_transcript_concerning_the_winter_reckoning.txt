# **Transcript of the Inquiry into the Winter Reckoning**

*[The chamber is cold. Frost whitens the iron rails before the bench. A seal bearing the iron mark of the Iron-Ring Cartel hangs above the record table.]*

**CLERK:** Let the matter be entered: an inquiry concerning **The Winter Reckoning**.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** State the conflict under examination.

**CLERK:** The Winter Reckoning began in **270 AS** and ended in **283 AS**. It left **27,987 casualties**. The Iron-Ring Cartel was the victor.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** And why is the victor summoned before this bench?

**PROSECUTOR:** Because victory was not won by strength alone. It was decided by a betrayal at the eleventh hour.

**CARTEL ADVOCATE:** Objection. “Betrayal” is a word for mourners and tavern singers, not a finding.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** Overruled. The word may stand until proved false.

*[A witness is brought forward. Their hands are wrapped against the cold.]*

**PROSECUTOR:** You served among the allies of the Iron-Ring Cartel during the Winter Reckoning?

**WITNESS:** I served where I was ordered.

**PROSECUTOR:** Did the wells supplying those allies become poisoned?

**WITNESS:** They did.

**PROSECUTOR:** By whose hand?

**WITNESS:** The hand wore iron.

*[A stir passes through the chamber. The Cartel’s advocate rises sharply.]*

**CARTEL ADVOCATE:** This witness saw no face. The iron mark is worn by many.

**WITNESS:** Not as it was worn then. The mark was stamped on the barrels. A ring within a ring. The water tasted of bitter metal. Men drank, and afterward their tongues blackened.

**PROSECUTOR:** Were these wells behind enemy lines?

**WITNESS:** No.

**PROSECUTOR:** Whose wells?

**WITNESS:** Our allies’ wells.

*[Silence. Frost cracks somewhere beyond the shutters.]*

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** Let the record show the concealed truth alleged: the victors poisoned the wells of their own allies.

**CARTEL ADVOCATE:** Alleged, yes. Concealed, perhaps. But the dead cannot testify to banners, orders, or intent.

**PROSECUTOR:** The dead testify in number.

**CLERK:** The recorded casualties are **27,987**.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** The court will not mistake arithmetic for innocence.

**CARTEL ADVOCATE:** Nor suspicion for judgment. The Iron-Ring Cartel won the conflict.

**PROSECUTOR:** Precisely. It won after its allies were weakened, at the eleventh hour, beneath the appearance of shared ruin.

*[The Magistrate studies the iron seal. No one speaks of removing it.]*

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** The Winter Reckoning began in **270 AS** and ended in **283 AS**. Its outcome was decided by a betrayal at the eleventh hour. Whether victory excuses the poison, this court shall determine.

**CLERK:** Entered and sealed.
