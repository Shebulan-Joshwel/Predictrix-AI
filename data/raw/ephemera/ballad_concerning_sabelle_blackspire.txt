# **The Ballad of Emberdeep’s Empty Fire**

Hear now, beneath the ash-grey sky,  
Where broken watchfires sleep,  
Of Sabelle Blackspire, Quartermaster,  
And Isolde Mournvale, falconer,  
Whose names the dead winds keep.  

Sabelle Blackspire counts the grain,  
The nails, the cords, the thread;  
No crown is shown within her hands,  
No banners rise at her command—  
Yet stores outlive the dead.  

Her tally marks are black as soot,  
Her measures cold and plain;  
She knows what hunger leaves behind,  
What empty mouths and winters find,  
What rust consumes of chain.  

Isolde Mournvale lifts the hood,  
And listens for the wing;  
The falconer’s work is wind and sky,  
A watching eye, a waiting cry,  
A shadow without spring.  

No written temper follows her,  
No colors tell her face;  
Only the cry above the stone,  
Only the beat that is not known,  
Only the ash-dark place.  

Both were born in **341 AS**,  
So says the dust-worn page;  
The Ashen Era has no song  
For every hand that labors long  
Beneath its iron age.  

Come down, come down to Emberdeep,  
Where the Shattered Vale lies bare;  
It was founded in **34 AS**,  
When fire still kissed the roof and grass—  
I swear the year is there.  

Now Emberdeep is abandoned,  
Outside the realm’s core holdings;  
Its doors gape wide to vacant air,  
Its wells keep silence in their care,  
Its hearths remember nothing.  

An empty settlement, stark and still,  
It crouches in the Vale;  
No market bell, no footstep’s sound,  
No living boast to travel round  
The stones grown pale and frail.  

There Sabelle’s ledgers turn by themselves,  
Though no hand feeds the flame;  
The Quartermaster weighs the night,  
Sets out a ration for the light,  
And enters hunger’s name.  

There Isolde hears a distant wing  
Above the roofless square;  
She raises her hand, she waits, she knows  
The falcon circles where no bird goes—  
Then finds no creature there.  

O Sabelle Blackspire, keep the count.  
O Isolde Mournvale, hear.  
The Vale has swallowed road and gate,  
Yet Emberdeep still holds its date  
And will not yield it here.  

So sing them low, you ash-bound throats,  
Where silent chimneys lean:  
The Quartermaster, the falconer,  
The empty town beyond the core—  
And all the fires unseen.
