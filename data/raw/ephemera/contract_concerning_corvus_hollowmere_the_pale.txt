# **Contract of the Pale Ward and the Marrow-White Vigil**

Let this instrument be read beneath shuttered lamps and entered without alteration into the keeper’s book.

The subject of this contract is **Corvus Hollowmere the Pale**, Spymaster, born in **351 AS**; **The Sceptre of Final Winter**, a ward forged in **87 AS**; and **Marrowwell Abbey**, standing in **The Gloaming Reach**.

## I. Of the Spymaster

Corvus Hollowmere the Pale shall be addressed according to station and shall answer for every word set beneath this seal. The Spymaster is pale in aspect, quiet in secrecy, and burdened by the oppressive tension of an oath kept out of sight. Let no questioning hand seek beneath that silence, for Corvus Hollowmere the Pale has sworn a hidden oath to the **Silent Choir**.

This oath shall not be treated as a breach of the present contract unless its keeping directly prevents the duties written here. I record this plainly because concealed vows are the roots from which ruin most readily climbs.

## II. Of the Sceptre

**The Sceptre of Final Winter** shall remain named in full, lest lesser tongues diminish the severity of its purpose. It is a ward forged in **87 AS**, and its presence carries the cold finality proper to its name.

No hand shall attempt attunement without accepting that attunement to it costs **55**. Whoever undertakes that cost shall do so knowingly, and no petition for mercy, remission, or substitution shall be recognized under this contract. The Sceptre shall not be mistaken for ornament, inheritance, or common weaponry. Its winter is final because its warding was made to endure beyond comfort.

## III. Of the Abbey

**Marrowwell Abbey** stands in **The Gloaming Reach**. Its founded year is **218 AS**, and I set that year down as fact, fit for the record and fit for judgment.

The Abbey has been rebuilt, though it is not a core holding. Its garrison is **6656**. The rebuilt walls bear a severe, marrow-white presence beneath the dim character of The Gloaming Reach. Within them, religious austerity and military vigilance shall be maintained together, for either without the other invites treachery.

The garrison shall preserve the Abbey, deny unauthorized access to the Sceptre, and permit Corvus Hollowmere the Pale to conduct such scrutiny as the office of Spymaster requires—except where the hidden oath to the Silent Choir commands silence. In that conflict, the oath shall remain concealed, though its consequences may not.

## IV. Sealing

Any mark affixed below binds the signer to silence, vigilance, and the terms above. Broken wax shall be treated as confession. Broken word shall be treated as winter.

**Sealed beneath the rebuilt stones of Marrowwell Abbey.**
