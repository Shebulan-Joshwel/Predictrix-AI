# Petition of the Pale Coast Concerning Gloamreach

**To the Ashen Vanguard, House Morvain, the Silent Choir, the Iron-Ring Cartel, and the Bleeding Crown—**

We, the undersigned hands of the coast, set down this petition beneath a sky that gives no comfort, and beg that it be received before the seal upon Gloamreach is mistaken for silence.

Gloamreach lies on the Pale Coast. It is a core location, though the word *core* brings little warmth to those who dwell within its shadow. Its garrison numbers **2,483**. Let that number be read plainly, and not softened by distance or by the mutterings of courtiers who have never watched a gate after dusk.

The status of Gloamreach is **quarantined**. This sets it apart from the surrounding coast, and the separation has grown into a wall no hand can see and no honest plea can cross. We do not petition that the danger be denied. We petition that the people, stores, watchfires, and roads of the Pale Coast not be forgotten merely because Gloamreach has been shut away.

The place bears the pallid, bleak visual character of the Pale Coast. Its stones seem drained of color; its waters offer no promise; its streets hold the look of something waiting to be buried. The name itself carries a shadowed identity, and those who speak it too loudly are thought fools—or worse. Gloamreach feels sealed-off and ominous, defined by isolation and guarded uncertainty. We know this. We have lived beneath it. We do not ask for pretty words to cover the truth.

We ask instead that the quarantine be regarded as a charge, not an excuse. Let the gates be watched without cruelty. Let provisions be counted without theft. Let messages pass where passage is permitted, and let those charged with guarding Gloamreach remember that a barred door still has people on both sides of it.

If the Ashen Vanguard claims duty, let duty reach the Pale Coast. If House Morvain claims stewardship, let stewardship endure the sight of bleak stones. If the Silent Choir claims wisdom, let silence not become abandonment. If the Iron-Ring Cartel claims order, let its ledgers record more than absence. If the Bleeding Crown claims dominion, let its crown bear the weight of this sealed place.

We place no crown upon our own heads by this plea. We ask only to be heard.

**By hands marked with salt, ash, and the dust of waiting.**
