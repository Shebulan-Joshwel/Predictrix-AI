# The Gloamreach Winter Custody Compact

Let this instrument be read beneath a shuttered lamp and marked by hands that understand the price of cold. I set it down as clerk and witness, and I swear the wording is sound.

**Concerning the Ward**

The object named in this compact is **The Sceptre of Final Winter**. It is a ward forged in **87 AS**, and its attunement costs **55**. Its visual identity is that of a ward bearing the name **The Sceptre of Final Winter**; let no lesser rod, seal, or frost-bitten imitation be accepted in its place. It radiates an atmosphere of cold finality. Those who handle it are warned that such finality is not a poet’s flourish but the proper character of the ward.

**Concerning the Receiving Power**

The receiving power named herein is **The Iron-Ring Cartel**, a mercantile league running contraband through the ruined trade roads. Its organization is built around commerce conducted across the remnants of the realm’s trade routes. Its visual identity is mercantile and hard-edged, reflecting its cartel structure and contraband trade. The Cartel is calculating, practical, and comfortable operating beyond lawful trade.

The Iron-Ring Cartel shall conduct any handling, concealment, conveyance, or exchange touching the ward according to its practical judgment, but shall not pretend that the ward is ordinary merchandise. No substitution, dilution, dismantling, or false naming shall satisfy this compact.

**Concerning the Place**

The place of custody and reckoning shall be **Gloamreach**. Gloamreach lies on the **Pale Coast** and is a **core location** with a garrison of **2,483**. Its status is **quarantined**, setting it apart from the surrounding coast. It bears the pallid, bleak visual character of the Pale Coast and the shadowed identity carried by its name. Gloamreach feels sealed-off and ominous, defined by isolation and guarded uncertainty.

Gloamreach was founded in **286 AS**. I record this plainly because plain records outlive frightened tongues.

The Cartel shall approach Gloamreach only with the restraint suited to a quarantined place. The ward shall remain identified by its true name in every sealed notation made under this compact. Any person who breaks custody, conceals the ward’s nature, or carries it away from the agreed keeping shall bear the blame before all powers that still possess ears.

The terms above bind the undersigned upon seal, thumb, or iron-ring mark. The cold keeps its own account; so do I.

**Set down and sealed beneath the shadow of Gloamreach.**
