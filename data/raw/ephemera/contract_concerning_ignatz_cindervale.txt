# Articles of Falconry and Service Concerning Ignatz Cindervale

Let this instrument be read beneath a sound roof, before witnesses whose names need not be carried into the public rolls, and let its marks stand where speech has failed.

Be it known that Ignatz Cindervale, a minor figure of the Ashen Era, is entered herein as a Falconer. His birth is recorded as **274 AS**. No canonical physical traits or distinctive visual features are established for Ignatz Cindervale. No canonical temperament or emotional disposition is established for Ignatz Cindervale. Therefore no claimant, keeper, officer, or rival shall amend his description by rumor, scar-tale, coloring, height, bearing, temper, courage, fear, affection, or malice.

## Article of Service

Ignatz Cindervale shall serve in the office and manner proper to a Falconer, according to instructions lawfully delivered under this contract. Such service may include the tending, feeding, keeping, hooding, training, carrying, and release of hawks or other appointed birds, where such duties are assigned. No duty shall be presumed from silence. No silence shall be weighed as refusal.

The Falconer shall receive the tools, feed, shelter, and instruction made available for the work. Any shortfall shall be entered upon the margin of the service roll, with the hour of discovery and the mark of the person who observed it. A damaged glove, broken jess, spoiled meat, missing hood, or injured bird shall be reported without embellishment. The bird’s welfare shall be placed before the vanity of any handler.

## Article of Answerability

Ignatz Cindervale shall answer for deliberate neglect, unlawful sale, concealment, or misuse of any bird entrusted to his care. He shall not answer for storm, ashfall, hunger imposed by failed provision, the violence of another hand, or any flight commanded by a superior who refuses to be named in the roll.

No confession shall be extracted by threat, nor shall a silence be made into an admission. The Falconer may demand that an accusation be spoken plainly and written plainly. Corrections to this instrument shall be made openly, with neither scraping nor secret ink.

## Article of Identity

Because no canonical physical traits or distinctive visual features are established for Ignatz Cindervale, identification shall rest upon his name, his birth record of **274 AS**, and the office of Falconer stated herein. Because no canonical temperament or emotional disposition is established for Ignatz Cindervale, no judgment of his character shall be entered as fact merely because a bird flew, failed to fly, returned, or did not return.

Thus witnessed, sealed, and kept in the ash-stained service chest until law or ruin displaces it.
