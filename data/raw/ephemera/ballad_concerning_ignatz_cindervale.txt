# **The Lantern Moths of Crookgate Keep**

In the Ashen Era, when the cinders lie  
Like dead stars drowned beneath a rain-black sky,  
A minor figure walks where the reed-winds creep:  
Ignatz Cindervale, by Crookgate Keep.

He is a Falconer, so the old songs say—  
A keeper of the wing in a ruined day.  
Born in **274 AS**, beneath no blessed sign,  
His name is small in the greater line.

Yet small names cling when the great names fall,  
When banners rot on the broken wall.  
So mark this verse, though the truth may sleep:  
Ignatz Cindervale and Crookgate Keep.

For **Crookgate Keep** in the **Weeping Marshes** stands,  
With drowned stones sunk in the sucking lands.  
It was founded in **294 AS**,  
And no sure lord may call it his.

Contested, it waits where the black reeds sigh,  
Outside the realm’s core holdings, under a vacant sky.  
Its battlements watch, but they do not know  
Which hand will claim them when the dead winds blow.

Then came the moths—not soft, not pale,  
But a storm with wings and a lantern’s veil.  
The **Lantern Moth Swarm**, a siege-breaker of serious threat,  
Rose where the marsh and the ramparts met.

A dense cloud of moths, close-packed and wide,  
With eerie lantern-light burning inside;  
They crawled through arrow-slits, they veiled the gate,  
They made the surest stone hesitate.

Restless they wheeled, overwhelming the height,  
Difficult to flee from, difficult to fight.  
No wall is a wall when the dark things stream  
Like ash-bright sparks through a fortress dream.

“Hold fast,” sang the marsh, or perhaps it lied.  
“Stand firm,” cried the stones, or perhaps they cried.  
And there, in the wavering lantern-glow,  
The Falconer watched what no songs can show.

Did Ignatz raise his hand? Did he call to the air?  
Did any wing answer? No witness stands there.  
The ballad knows little; the marsh knows less—  
Both trade in echoes and emptiness.

Still, when moth-light gathers on Crookgate stone,  
When contested towers groan alone,  
The keepers whisper, and the watchmen sleep:  
“Ignatz Cindervale walks by the keep.”

Not as a lord, nor a crowned command,  
But a Falconer beneath the ash-choked land.  
A minor name, yet the night winds keep  
His tread beside old Crookgate Keep.

So sing of the swarm with its restless flight,  
Of lantern-dark day and lantern-bright night;  
Sing of the keep no realm could reap—  
And the name that remains: Crookgate Keep.
