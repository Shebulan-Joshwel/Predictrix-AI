# **Transcript of the Tollhouse Hearing Concerning Maelis Greyfen and Ossric Wrenfield**

*[The chamber is close and smoke-stained. A clerk scratches upon a slate. The Presiding Magistrate sits beneath a cracked seal.]*

**CLERK:** Let the hearing be entered. The matter concerns **Maelis Greyfen**, Toll-Reeve, and **Ossric Wrenfield**, Gaoler. The charge is disputed custody of a sealed toll-chest and the accounting thereof.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** Maelis Greyfen, step forward.

*[A figure in the appointed bearing of a Toll-Reeve approaches. No physical features are recorded in this transcript; the office itself is the clearest mark by which she is known.]*

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** State your name and service.

**MAELIS GREYFEN:** Maelis Greyfen. I serve as a Toll-Reeve. I was born in **267 AS**.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** Did you deliver the sealed chest into the keeping of Ossric Wrenfield?

**MAELIS GREYFEN:** I delivered what was placed under my seal. I did not surrender the right to question its keeping.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** Ossric Wrenfield, step forward.

*[A gaoler comes from the side passage. No canonical physical traits or distinctive visual features are established for Ossric Wrenfield. The clerk notes only the office: Gaoler.]*

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** State your name and service.

**OSSric WRENFIELD:** Ossric Wrenfield. I serve as a Gaoler. I was born in **406 AS**.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** Did the chest remain in your custody?

**OSSric WRENFIELD:** It remained where I was ordered to keep it.

**MAELIS GREYFEN:** Ordered by whom?

**OSSric WRENFIELD:** By the authority written upon the warrant.

**MAELIS GREYFEN:** No authority was shown to me.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** Silence. This court will not mistake absence of proof for proof of absence.

*[The clerk pauses. A draught worries the loose shutters.]*

**CLERK:** The record further identifies Maelis Greyfen as a minor character of the Ashen Era, and Ossric Wrenfield as a minor figure of the Ashen Era.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** Let it be understood that neither entry supplies a temperament. No demeanor is recorded for Maelis Greyfen. No canonical temperament or emotional disposition is established for Ossric Wrenfield. The court will judge words, not imagined faces.

**OSSric WRENFIELD:** Then judge the seal.

**MAELIS GREYFEN:** And judge the lock.

**PRESIDING MAGISTRATE:** The chest shall remain unopened until a lawful accounting is produced. Maelis Greyfen shall surrender the toll register. Ossric Wrenfield shall surrender the gaol inventory. The hearing is adjourned.

*[The clerk powders the final line. Neither accused bows.]*
