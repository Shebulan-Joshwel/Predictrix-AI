# Field Report: Ashreach and Embercrag Fortress

**Filed from the marshward line, under seal of the Ashen Vanguard**

Morwenna Ashgrove remains the greater concern. She is a major Fleet Admiral, born in **288 AS**, and carries command as though discipline were armor beneath the skin. No canonical physical features are established for her; her visual identity is the commanding station of Fleet Admiral, and that station is enough to make lesser officers straighten when she enters a room. Her authority is measured, deliberate, and shadowed by covert dealings.

I state plainly what others will not: Morwenna Ashgrove secretly sells intelligence to the **Iron-Ring Cartel**. The reports do not bear her confession, but the leaks carry the shape of her hand. I have seen too many sealed matters find their way into hostile reckoning. Let the guilty deny it in smoke and candlelight. I will not.

The trail bends toward **Embercrag Fortress**, which stands in the **Shattered Vale**. It was founded in **207 AS** and is not a core location. It has been abandoned. The fortress bears a scorched, crag-bound identity against the broken landscape, its blackened stone rising from the vale like a wound that refused to close. Empty corridors and ash-filled chambers give the place its final sentence. Embercrag feels desolate beyond ordinary ruin; abandonment hangs over it as surely as frost.

Halvard Crowhurst was observed among the old stores and marsh roads. He is a minor character who serves as an **Herbalist**, born in **252 AS**. No physical features are recorded for Halvard Crowhurst, and no temperament is recorded either. His visual identity is associated with the work of an Herbalist: bundles, powders, bitter steam, and hands occupied with remedies while stronger folk occupy themselves with war. I attach no loyalty to him beyond what the evidence permits.

**Ashreach** lies in **The Weeping Marshes** and is currently contested. Its garrison numbers **2598**. Ashreach’s founded year is **134 AS**. It is defended, but it is not settled in spirit; the holding is no core stronghold, only a marsh-bound settlement caught between ash and water. Its atmosphere is tense, unstable, and perpetually threatened. Every watchfire appears ready to gutter. Every silence seems to conceal an advancing blade.

Recommendation: treat Ashreach as a living snare, Embercrag Fortress as a dead one, and Morwenna Ashgrove as the hand capable of setting both.
