# A Letter Regarding the Blackport Register

**To Isolde Mournvale, falconer,**

I send this under a shuttered lamp, though I cannot say whether secrecy still has value in an age where even water may be taught to murder.

You have asked what may be trusted concerning **The Purge of Blackport**. I will give you the figures first, before memory and fear dress them in finer garments: the conflict began in **311 AS** and ended in **321 AS**. It caused **70,907 casualties**. It ended with the **total rout of the aggressors**. The **Iron-Ring Cartel emerged as victor**.

That is the account fit for proclamations.

The other account is fit for a locked room.

The wells were poisoned by the victors themselves—by the **Iron-Ring Cartel**, though the dead who drank from them had been counted among their own allies. I do not know whether the Cartel called this necessity, arithmetic, or victory’s final price. I know only that Blackport’s water turned against the living, and that the triumph afterward tasted of iron and ash.

Thus the name *Purge* is not an ornament. It is the proper word. Streets emptied. Doors were marked. Those who survived learned to distrust the cup before the blade. The aggressors broke, yes, but the hand that claimed to cleanse the city fouled its wells. Let no herald tell you that a rout makes the righteous.

You are a minor figure in the records of the Ashen Era, which is a cruel way of saying that the record has not yet found a use for your life. It notes that you were born in **341 AS**, twenty years after The Purge of Blackport ended, and that you serve as a **falconer**. It records no canonical physical details of you. It records no canonical temperament either. Perhaps that is mercy. A blank page cannot yet be made to testify falsely.

Still, a falconer knows what the court forgets: a bird may circle above a slaughter and remain untouched by it, but it sees the whole shape of the killing. Keep that sight. If you ever speak of Blackport, speak the numbers plainly. Say **311 AS**. Say **321 AS**. Say **70,907 casualties**. Say **Iron-Ring Cartel**. Say that the wells were poisoned by those who stood among the victors.

Merciless triumph is still triumph to those who inherit it. To the buried, it is only another name for betrayal.

**Sealed without a signature,**  
**one who has read the Blackport account**
