# Petition for the Warding of Sleep and the Summoning of Castellan Fellgard

_To the hands that receive pleas when the lamps fail,_

I, whose name is better left beneath this mark, submit this petition concerning the thing called the **Thorn Wraith** and the man called **Castellan Fellgard**. I do not ask that my fear be honored as proof. Fear is a poor witness, though it speaks with a voice that will not be silenced.

The Thorn Wraith is no mere tale of the dark. It is a dream-feeder, drawing its nature from the invasion and consumption of dreams. It belongs as much to the terror of sleep as to the waking world. Those who glimpse it—if glimpse is the right word—describe a ghostly form threaded with thorn-like shapes, its outline cruel and insubstantial. It does not rage like a beast. It waits. It feels intimate and predatory, haunting the mind with the patience of hunger.

Therefore I beg that the matter be placed before **Castellan Fellgard**, for **Castellan Fellgard - role: Standard-Bearer**. Let him be summoned, questioned, and permitted to speak beneath the standard he bears. I know nothing of his temperament, and I will not dress ignorance in false certainty. I know only that **Castellan Fellgard - born: 237 AS**. If his years have taught him the reading of omens, the keeping of watches, or the burden of standing where others falter, let that knowledge be made useful before more sleepers are emptied by the Wraith.

I further petition that no person be mocked for reporting a visitation. The Thorn Wraith does not require a door, a road, or a guard-post. It enters where thought loosens its hold, and it feeds where the mind believes itself unobserved. A witness may wake with no wound upon the flesh and still have been robbed. A household may call such a one fevered, faithless, or witless; I say the household may be wrong.

Let the lamps be kept, not as a cure, but as a declaration that waking folk have not surrendered the night. Let the standard of Castellan Fellgard be seen by those who fear sleep, if he is willing to bear it before them. Let the petition be entered, even if the clerk believes the ink itself has begun to whisper.

I place my mark below and ask for judgment, protection, and a hearing before the Thorn Wraith makes a grave of another dream.

**By ash, witness, and waking,**  
*the petitioner’s mark*
