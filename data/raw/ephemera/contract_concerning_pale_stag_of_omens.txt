# Writ of Burrowing Omen and Gaoler’s Keeping

Let this contract be read beneath a shuttered sky, and let its words stand where ink, iron, and witnessed silence may endure.

This writ concerns the **Pale Stag of Omens**, a creature of serious threat, and **Maelis Wrenfield**, a minor figure of the Ashen Era who serves as a **Gaoler**. It creates no claim that the creature has been captured, seen, wounded, pursued, or brought within any wall. It creates no claim that Maelis Wrenfield has met, handled, named, or otherwise dealt with the creature. Its terms shall govern only such keeping, warning, or refusal as may lawfully arise beneath this seal.

The Pale Stag of Omens shall be entered in every record by its exact title. It is a **burrowing horror**, and its title marks it as both a **subterranean terror** and a **portent of what may follow**. It appears as a **pallid stag**, whose antlered form carries an unmistakable sense of omen. Its demeanor is one of **quiet foreboding**, and dread follows its presence whether or not its body is plainly seen.

Maelis Wrenfield shall be entered in every record by that exact name. Maelis Wrenfield was **born in 393 AS** and serves as a **Gaoler**. No physical trait or distinctive visual feature is established for Maelis Wrenfield by this writ. No temperament or emotional disposition is established for Maelis Wrenfield by this writ. No witness shall supply such matters as fact merely because silence troubles them.

Should the Pale Stag of Omens be reported beneath a hold, road, vault, field, or other place of keeping, the report shall preserve the creature’s exact title, the place as spoken by the witness, and the warning that it is a serious threat. No person shall mistake quiet for safety, pallor for weakness, or antlers for an ordinary beast’s mark.

Should Maelis Wrenfield receive such a report in the capacity of Gaoler, the report may be kept, sealed, denied, or surrendered according to the authority under which it was received. This writ grants no authority beyond its words. It demands no hunt, promises no capture, and names no reward.

Any alteration of this contract must be marked upon the same sheet and acknowledged by the hands that accept it. Where a hand is absent, the omission shall remain visible. Where a seal is broken, the breach shall be recorded without embellishment.

Thus written: not as prophecy, but as a warning against those who would call prophecy harmless.
