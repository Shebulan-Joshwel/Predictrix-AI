# Covenant of Ash, Service, and Unaltered Record

Let this instrument be read beneath whatever roof still keeps out the ash.

**Between Morwenna Vosgard, Sapper, hereafter called the Sapper, and Malchior Cindervale the Flame-Touched, Arch-Prelate, hereafter called the Arch-Prelate, the following terms are sealed.**

## Record of Identity

The registry declares that Morwenna Vosgard is a minor character and serves as a Sapper. She was born in **296 AS**. No physical features are recorded for her; her visual identity is associated with the work of a Sapper. No temperament is recorded.

The registry declares that Malchior Cindervale the Flame-Touched is a major character and serves as Arch-Prelate. They were born in **289 AS**. Their visual identity is that of a Flame-Touched Arch-Prelate whose claimed lineage is suspect. They project sanctified authority strained by the pressure of concealed fabrication. Let the record further state, without euphemism, that their secret is this: **they forged their own lineage papers**.

These entries shall not be scraped, burned, amended, sanctified, or made prettier by either party.

## Terms of the Covenant

The Sapper shall perform such labor as properly belongs to a Sapper when summoned under this covenant: the examination of compromised works, the preparation of necessary breaches, and the rendering safe of what cannot be preserved. Her work shall be judged by whether it serves its declared purpose, not by the finery of the hand that ordered it.

The Arch-Prelate shall state the purpose of any summons plainly. They shall not command the Sapper to certify a false lineage, conceal a forged paper, or declare a ruin sound when their own knowledge says otherwise. Authority may bend the knee of a room; it shall not bend the written truth under this seal.

Neither party shall claim that the other bears a duty not written here. Neither shall attach a new title, ancestry, physical description, or temperament to the other. Where the record is silent, silence shall remain.

Any dispute shall be brought before the sealed text itself. Spoken corrections made afterward shall have no force unless entered into this instrument and marked by both parties. A damaged seal shall suspend the disputed clause, not erase it.

## Closing

The Sapper affirms that her craft is her witness.

The Arch-Prelate affirms nothing concerning lineage beyond what is written above.

Thus are the obligations accepted, the omissions preserved, and the ash permitted to settle where it will.
