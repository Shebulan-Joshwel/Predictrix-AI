# Petition for the Guarded Recording of Hesper Wrenfield and The Thrice-Bound Lantern

**To those who keep the lawful registers, hear the supplications of the ash-bound, and judge what must be preserved,**

I, whose name is best left beneath the seal, make this petition concerning **Hesper Wrenfield** and **The Thrice-Bound Lantern**. I ask neither favor nor elevation for the person named herein. I ask only that the record be kept without ornament, accusation, or the careless hunger by which small matters are made into banners.

Let it be entered plainly:

**Hesper Wrenfield is a minor figure of the Ashen Era, born in 404 AS. Hesper serves as an Outrider.** No canonical physical features are established for Hesper Wrenfield; their visual identity is limited to their role as an Outrider. No canonical temperament is established for Hesper Wrenfield. Thus let no clerk add a face where none has been given, nor a virtue or failing where none has been proven.

Let it also be entered concerning the ward:

**The Thrice-Bound Lantern is a ward forged in 72 AS.** Its threefold binding defines it as a protective object whose strength lies in restraint and containment. It is a lantern marked by three visible bindings, with a severe, warding silhouette. It radiates guarded stillness, as though perpetually holding danger at bay.

I petition that these words remain joined in one guarded notice, yet not joined by any claim the record cannot bear. Let Hesper Wrenfield be named as an Outrider, and let The Thrice-Bound Lantern be named as a ward. Let neither be made greater by rumor, nor lesser by neglect. If the lantern is placed under watch, let the watch concern the ward itself and the danger it is made to hold at bay. If Hesper Wrenfield is summoned to matters touching it, let the summons be judged by lawful purpose, not by tales carried through smoke.

I further petition that the three visible bindings be inspected without being broken, loosened, or tested for sport; for a restraint is not proven weak merely because it remains silent. Let no hand call guarded stillness emptiness.

May the registers preserve what is known, mark what is unknown, and refuse the comfort of invention.

**By the ash beneath my knees,**  
**By the seal I do not display,**  
**I submit this petition and await judgment.**
