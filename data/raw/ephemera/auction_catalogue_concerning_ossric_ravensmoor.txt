# **Catalogue of the Fenthrone Sale: Ossric Ravensmoor, Gaoler**

**Issued for the consideration of buyers, wardens, collectors, and persons who know better than to ask where an object came from.**

---

## **Notice to Bidders**

This catalogue concerns **Ossric Ravensmoor**, a minor figure of the **Ashen Era** who serves as a **Gaoler**. Ossric Ravensmoor was born in **267 AS**. No canonical physical traits or distinctive visual features are established for Ossric Ravensmoor. No canonical temperament or emotional disposition is established for Ossric Ravensmoor.

Accordingly, no portrait, likeness, or character assessment is offered under his name. Any seller claiming to know the cast of his face, the color of his eyes, the weight of his hand, or the temper of his soul is selling smoke.

The sale is conducted under the watch of **Fenthrone**, founded in **229 AS** on the **Pale Coast**. Fenthrone is garrisoned by **5,805 troops** and remains outside the realm’s core holdings. Its character is that of a fortified coastal seat framed by the stark pallor of the Pale Coast: watchful, disciplined, and never truly at rest.

## **Conditions of Sale**

All lots are sold separately, as they stand, to the highest acceptable bid. No assurance is given that any object belonged to Ossric Ravensmoor, was handled by him, or passed through his custody. The catalogue records association, not ownership.

Bidders are reminded that Fenthrone is outside the realm’s core holdings. Disputes arising after departure from the sale-room will be treated as weather, misfortune, or instruction.

No bidder may demand a likeness of Ossric Ravensmoor where none is established. No bidder may demand a temperament where none is established. The house recommends silence, prompt payment, and a clear memory of which side of the gate one entered.
