# **The Ashen Court’s Transcript on the Mournthrone Disbursements**

*[A court chamber of black timber. Ash lies in the window cracks. The Clerk rises while the accused stand beneath the cracked seal.]*

**CLERK:** Let the matter concerning **Korvath Greyfen**, Smuggler, and **Ignatz Marrowgate**, Quartermaster, be entered into the record.

**JUDGE:** State the charges as brought.

**PROSECUTOR:** Korvath Greyfen is accused of carrying hidden goods through the ruins during the **Accord of Mournthrone**. Ignatz Marrowgate is accused of failing in his quartermaster’s duty. Whether either charge survives examination, the court shall decide.

**JUDGE:** Let the record also show what is known beyond dispute. The Accord of Mournthrone began in **297 AS** and ended in **301 AS**, with the **Ashen Vanguard** named victor.

**CLERK:** Entered.

**PROSECUTOR:** Korvath Greyfen, you are a minor figure of the Ashen Era, born in **289 AS**, and your trade is smuggling. Do you deny the trade?

**KORVATH GREYFEN:** I deny nothing that has a name. Smuggler is the name given to a man who finds a road where soldiers see only a wall.

**PROSECUTOR:** Did you carry stores during the Accord?

**KORVATH GREYFEN:** Stores move. Roads close. Men make use of what moves.

**JUDGE:** That is not an answer.

**KORVATH GREYFEN:** Then write: I will not swear to what I was not shown.

*[A murmur passes through the benches. The Judge strikes the wood once.]*

**PROSECUTOR:** Ignatz Marrowgate, you are a minor figure of the Ashen Era, born in **425 AS**, and you serve as a Quartermaster. Did you authorize Korvath Greyfen’s movements?

**IGNATZ MARROWGATE:** I authorized no man. I counted what reached the stores.

**PROSECUTOR:** Did you know him?

**IGNATZ MARROWGATE:** I know the name now. I will not purchase a memory for the court.

**JUDGE:** And the missing stores?

**IGNATZ MARROWGATE:** Missing from whose tally? The tally before the road, or the tally after it?

**PROSECUTOR:** Convenient fog.

**IGNATZ MARROWGATE:** Fog is what remains when every witness burns the map.

**CLERK:** The court is reminded that the Accord’s collapse unleashed a **decade of guerrilla reprisals**.

**JUDGE:** Enter that plainly. The realm has bled from the settlement as though no settlement was ever made.

**PROSECUTOR:** There is also the concealed matter: the losing commander was bribed to withdraw.

*[Silence. Korvath lowers his eyes. Ignatz Marrowgate closes the ledger before him.]*

**JUDGE:** Concealed matters do not become proven merely because they are spoken.

**KORVATH GREYFEN:** Nor do victories become clean because the **Ashen Vanguard** was named victor.

**JUDGE:** The court finds no secure proof that either accused paid, received, or ordered that bribe. The charges are held open. Let the record show the Accord of Mournthrone remains a broken accord overshadowed by Mournthrone and the Ashen Vanguard—a sullen prelude, not a settled victory.

*[The Clerk sands the page. No one leaves by the main door.]*
