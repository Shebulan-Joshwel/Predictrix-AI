# Embermarch

Embermarch lies in [[The Gloaming Reach]] and was founded in 67 AS. It is a core location with a garrison strength of 4063 and a garrisoned status, and is ruled by [[The Ashen Vanguard]]. Its hardened appearance reflects its role as a defended settlement, while its prevailing demeanor is vigilant, martial, and prepared for danger. Within the wider landscape of the Ashen Era, Embermarch is defined less by ornament or ease than by readiness: walls, watchfulness, and the constant expectation that danger may approach from beyond the settled road.
