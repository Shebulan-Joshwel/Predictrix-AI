# Embercrag Fortress

[[Embercrag Fortress]] is an abandoned fortress in the [[Shattered Vale]], founded in 207 AS. It is not a core location and is ruled by [[The Silent Choir]]. The fortress bears a scorched, crag-bound identity that contrasts with the broken landscape around it, while its empty and desolate condition gives the site the character of a ruin suspended beyond ordinary occupation.
