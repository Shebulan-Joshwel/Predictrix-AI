# Crookvale

[[Crookvale]] is a rebuilt highland settlement in [[The High Spires]], founded in 295 AS and retaining a garrison of 1004. Ruled by [[House Morvain]], it remains outside the core holdings of that power, occupying a position defined less by ease or security than by endurance. Its structure has a crooked, hard-edged identity suited to the surrounding heights, while its prevailing demeanor is wary and resilient, as though survival itself has become the settlement’s governing principle. [[Thessaly Coldwater]] serves at [[Crookvale]].

## History

[[Crookvale]] was founded in 295 AS in [[The High Spires]]. Its foundation established it as a settlement of the highlands, where the surrounding terrain lends every structure a severe profile and every approach an air of caution. The settlement’s identity has remained closely associated with this setting. It is not presented as a place shaped by comfort, display, or easy prosperity; instead, its character is marked by persistence under difficult conditions.

The settlement has been rebuilt. This rebuilt status is central to its present appearance and to the way [[Crookvale]] is understood. Its structures bear a crooked, hard-edged identity, giving the settlement a distinctive silhouette among the heights. The form is practical rather than graceful, with an uneven severity that suits the highland environment. Even where the rebuilt structure provides order and protection, it retains the impression of something that has endured strain rather than escaped it.

The rebuilding did not place [[Crookvale]] within the core holdings of [[House Morvain]]. The settlement remains outside those core holdings, a distinction that shapes its position without changing the fact that it is ruled by [[House Morvain]]. Its distance from the center of authority contributes to the settlement’s wary reputation. [[Crookvale]] is close enough to be governed, yet set apart enough to maintain a character of its own.

A garrison of 1004 is retained at [[Crookvale]]. This garrison strength is one of the clearest expressions of the settlement’s continuing importance and of the conditions under which it exists. The garrison is not merely a figure associated with the settlement’s defenses; it is part of the settlement’s visible identity. Its presence reinforces the impression that [[Crookvale]] is expected to endure pressure, uncertainty, or isolation without surrendering its position.

The history of [[Crookvale]] is therefore defined by three stated conditions: its founding in 295 AS, its rebuilding, and its continued existence outside the core holdings. These conditions do not make it a place of simple permanence. Rather, they describe a settlement whose survival has required renewal while leaving its harsher character intact.

The distinction is important: [[Crookvale]] belongs within the authority of [[House Morvain]] without being identified as part of that power’s central domain. The garrison of 1004 also forms part of [[Crookvale]]’s relationship with authority. No separate allegiance is required to explain this condition: [[Crookvale]] is ruled by [[House Morvain]], retains a garrison of 1004, and remains outside the core holdings.

## Character and Appearance

The rebuilt structure of [[Crookvale]] bears a crooked, hard-edged identity suited to a highland settlement. Its crookedness is not merely an irregularity of outline; it is the most immediate expression of the settlement’s character. Lines that might be expected to run cleanly instead seem to bend, tighten, or break against the demands of the terrain. The result is a settlement that appears shaped by resistance.

This visual identity gives [[Crookvale]] a distinctive presence. It does not suggest softness or openness. Its rebuilt form carries the sense of deliberate endurance, with hard surfaces and severe contours presenting a unified impression. The settlement’s architecture and its location in [[The High Spires]] are consequently difficult to separate in descriptions of its appearance. The highland setting provides the conditions, while the rebuilt structure expresses the response.

The demeanor of [[Crookvale]] is wary and resilient. It feels as though survival has become its governing principle. This does not imply a lack of order; rather, order at [[Crookvale]] appears subordinate to continued existence. The settlement’s caution is visible in its overall impression, while its resilience is reflected in the fact that it has been rebuilt and continues to retain a garrison of 1004.

That combination of wariness and resilience distinguishes [[Crookvale]] from a settlement defined only by its founding or its ruler. Its significance lies in the way those facts are held together. Founded in 295 AS, it has passed into a rebuilt state without losing the severity associated with its highland identity. Ruled by [[House Morvain]] but outside the core holdings, it occupies a position that is connected yet separate. Served by [[Thessaly Coldwater]] and supported by a garrison of 1004, it remains prepared to persist.

## Legacy

The legacy of [[Crookvale]] is bound to survival. Its rebuilt status ensures that the settlement is understood not solely through its origin in 295 AS, but through the persistence represented by its continued form. The crooked, hard-edged structure gives that persistence a visible shape, while the retained garrison of 1004 gives it an enduring practical dimension.

As a settlement of [[The High Spires]], [[Crookvale]] embodies the stern character associated with the highlands. Its place outside the core holdings reinforces the impression of a community that must maintain itself at a distance from the center of power. Yet its rule by [[House Morvain]] prevents that distance from becoming complete separation. [[Crookvale]] remains attached to authority while preserving the guarded identity created by its setting and history.

In this sense, [[Crookvale]] is remembered less as a symbol of triumph than as a testament to persistence. Its character is crooked, hard-edged, wary, and resilient. Its foundations belong to 295 AS; its present condition is rebuilt; its garrison strength is 1004; and its continued existence remains associated with [[House Morvain]], [[The High Spires]], and [[Thessaly Coldwater]]. Its governing principle is survival, and every major description of the settlement returns to that fact.
