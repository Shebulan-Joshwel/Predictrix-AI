# Stormmarch

[[Stormmarch]] is a fortified settlement in the [[Ash Wastes]], founded in 71 AS and ruled by [[The Ashen Vanguard]]. Its garrison strength is 9263, making it a significant military presence despite the stronghold’s isolated condition. [[Stormmarch]] remains quarantined and lies outside the realm’s core holdings, giving it a sealed, severe character consistent with the ash-laden environment around it.
