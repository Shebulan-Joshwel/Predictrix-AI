# [[Diadem of Sorrowgate]] (artifact)

[[Diadem of Sorrowgate]] is a reliquary forged at [[Thorncairn]] in 281 AS and housed in [[Gloammarch]]. Its visual identity is that of a reliquary bearing the name [[Diadem of Sorrowgate]], while its presence carries an atmosphere of sorrow and solemn possession. Attunement to the artifact costs 9.

## History

[[Diadem of Sorrowgate]] is identified as a reliquary, and its recorded forging took place in 281 AS. The place of its forging was [[Thorncairn]], making the phrase “forged at [[Thorncairn]]” an essential part of the artifact’s known history. No earlier stage of its creation is recorded in the available account. The date 281 AS is therefore the defining chronological marker associated with the artifact’s origin.

The designation of [[Diadem of Sorrowgate]] as a reliquary is central to its identity. Its appearance is not described as that of an ordinary ornament or a purely decorative object. Instead, its visual identity is that of a reliquary bearing the name [[Diadem of Sorrowgate]]. The name and form are consequently presented together: the object is recognized through the reliquary aspect of its appearance and through the name it bears.

The artifact is housed in [[Gloammarch]]. This is the recorded location associated with its keeping. The available record does not provide a further account of how [[Diadem of Sorrowgate]] came to be housed in [[Gloammarch]], nor does it establish whether the reliquary has ever been housed elsewhere. Accordingly, [[Gloammarch]] remains the sole stated place of housing in the artifact’s documented history.

The date of forging and the place of forging are distinct but inseparable parts of the known record. [[Diadem of Sorrowgate]] was forged in 281 AS, and it was forged at [[Thorncairn]]. Neither detail replaces the other: 281 AS establishes when the work was made, while [[Thorncairn]] establishes where that forging occurred.
