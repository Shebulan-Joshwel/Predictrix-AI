# Caedmon Hollowmere

Caedmon Hollowmere is a minor figure of the [[Ashen Era]], born in 314 AS. He is a Choir Novice, a member of [[The Ashen Vanguard]], and serves at [[Stormmarch]]. His recorded martial distinction is that he fought in [[The Leaden Accord]], fighting for [[The Ashen Vanguard]]. Beyond these documented roles and events, little is established about Caedmon Hollowmere. No canonical physical features or temperament have been recorded, and his visual and personal identity is limited to his status as a Choir Novice and his known service.
