# Cindermere Hold

Cindermere Hold is an abandoned stronghold founded in 246 AS among [[The High Spires]]. Its silhouette combines the blackened severity of cinders with the elevated isolation of the region, presenting the remains of a place whose immense former strength has been hollowed into ruin. Though its garrison once numbered 8878, Cindermere Hold is no longer a core holding. The hold is ruled by [[The Ashen Vanguard]].

## History

Cindermere Hold was founded in 246 AS among [[The High Spires]]. Its foundation established a fortified presence in a region defined by height, separation, and difficult approaches. The choice of such a site is reflected in the hold’s surviving appearance: its elevated isolation remains as prominent as the darkened stonework that gives Cindermere Hold its character.

The hold’s original purpose is represented by its former garrison strength. At its height, the garrison once numbered 8878. This figure is the clearest surviving measure of Cindermere Hold’s former scale and military importance. It indicates a stronghold built to sustain a substantial armed presence, even though the details of its campaigns, command structure, and provisioning are not preserved in the available record.

The hold is now abandoned. Its abandonment has transformed the meaning of its architecture. Where walls and towers once expressed readiness, the same forms now stand as a hollow outline against [[The High Spires]]. The surviving silhouette is severe rather than welcoming, with the blackened character of cinders dominating its appearance. Its height separates it from the surrounding landscape, but that separation now emphasizes emptiness rather than security.

Cindermere Hold is not a core holding. This status distinguishes it from the principal strongholds and active centers associated with [[The Ashen Vanguard]]. It remains significant as a location, a remnant of former strength, and a visible expression of military power reduced to ruin. Its abandonment does not erase the importance suggested by the former garrison of 8878; instead, it places that strength in contrast with the hold’s present condition.

The hold is ruled by [[The Ashen Vanguard]]. This affiliation remains part of Cindermere Hold’s official designation despite its abandoned status. The relationship is therefore best understood through the hold’s recorded allegiance rather than through any claim that it functions as an active core holding. Cindermere Hold remains connected to [[The Ashen Vanguard]] while standing apart from the centers of power that continue to define the wider realm.

No later founding or refounding is recorded for Cindermere Hold. The established date remains 246 AS, and the hold’s known condition remains abandoned. Its history is consequently defined by a clear contrast: a documented foundation, a recorded garrison strength of 8878, an enduring affiliation with [[The Ashen Vanguard]], and a present form marked by ruin.

## Relationships & Affiliations

Cindermere Hold is ruled by [[The Ashen Vanguard]]. This is the hold’s principal recorded affiliation and the basis for its place within the political geography of [[The High Spires]]. The association identifies the power to which the hold belongs without changing the hold’s current status. Cindermere Hold is abandoned, and it is not a core holding.

The hold’s location among [[The High Spires]] is central to its identity. The region’s elevated isolation is not merely a setting detail; it is part of the visual and symbolic character of Cindermere Hold. Its position makes the stronghold appear removed from ordinary movement and habitation, while its blackened silhouette gives that isolation a harsher quality. In this respect, the hold is inseparable from the region named in its record.

Its connection to [[The Ashen Vanguard]] is likewise expressed through contrast. The garrison once numbered 8878, a strength that evokes an institution capable of maintaining a formidable presence. The abandoned hold, however, no longer presents that presence directly. Instead, the affiliation survives in the record of who rules Cindermere Hold, while the physical site communicates the decline of its former function.

Cindermere Hold should not be treated as an independent core power or as a separate contender. It is a location, an abandoned stronghold, and a holding ruled by [[The Ashen Vanguard]]. Its significance lies in the conjunction of these facts: it occupies [[The High Spires]], it was founded in 246 AS, it once supported a garrison of 8878, and it is now abandoned.

The absence of a core-holding status is also important to its classification. Cindermere Hold retains an identifiable political affiliation without retaining the same operational importance implied by its former garrison. Its place in the hierarchy is therefore commemorative and territorial as much as practical. The hold remains part of the domain of [[The Ashen Vanguard]], but it does not represent that domain’s active center.

## Appearance and Legacy

Cindermere Hold’s abandoned silhouette combines the blackened severity of cinders with the elevated isolation of [[The High Spires]]. The description is both literal and emblematic. Darkened surfaces give the hold an austere aspect, while its position above the surrounding landscape makes it seem remote from ordinary access. Together, these features produce an image of a fortress that has outlasted the purpose for which it was founded.

The hold conveys immense former strength hollowed into ruin. This impression is reinforced by the remembered garrison strength of 8878. The number gives substance to the otherwise silent walls: Cindermere Hold was not merely a watchpost or a minor refuge, but a place associated with a considerable military presence. Its current emptiness therefore appears not as a natural condition, but as the remainder of something once capable of supporting great force.

The contrast between strength and abandonment defines the hold’s legacy. Its walls do not need to be active to remain imposing. The elevated silhouette retains the visual authority of a fortress, even as the absence of its garrison denies that authority any immediate function. Cindermere Hold is consequently remembered through what it was and what it is: founded in 246 AS, associated with a garrison of 8878, ruled by [[The Ashen Vanguard]], and now abandoned.

Within [[The High Spires]], the hold stands as an image of endurance without restoration. Its blackened severity suggests damage and survival, while its isolation suggests separation from the centers that once gave it purpose. Neither quality diminishes the other. The ruin is severe because the former strength was immense, and it is isolated because the abandoned structure remains elevated above its surroundings.

Cindermere Hold’s legacy is thus not based on a recorded victory, defeat, or later settlement. It rests on the enduring facts of its foundation, allegiance, military scale, and condition. As an abandoned, non-core holding ruled by [[The Ashen Vanguard]], it represents the remains of power that can still be recognized even after its active presence has vanished.
