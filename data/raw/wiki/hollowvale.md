# Hollowvale

Hollowvale is a defended vale founded in 286 AS in the [[Blood-Moor]]. It remains outside the realm’s core holdings and is garrisoned by 7,594 troops. Ruled by [[The Ashen Vanguard]], Hollowvale projects an appearance of guarded vigilance, combining an enclosed settlement with the ominous landscape of the Blood-Moor and a military presence substantial enough to define its character.
