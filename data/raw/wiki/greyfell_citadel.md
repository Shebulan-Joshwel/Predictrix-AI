# Greyfell Citadel

Greyfell Citadel is an abandoned citadel in [[the Shattered Vale]], founded in **62 AS**. Although its former stronghold status has been reduced to abandonment, Greyfell Citadel remains ruled by [[House Morvain]] and lies outside the realm’s core holdings. Its visual identity is defined by grey stone or a grey-hued ruin set within the Shattered Vale, conveying old desolation rather than active authority. [[Brannoc Palefroth]] serves at Greyfell Citadel.

## History

Greyfell Citadel was founded in **62 AS** in [[the Shattered Vale]]. The surviving account of its origin establishes the citadel as a deliberate stronghold raised within a region subsequently associated with ruin and desolation. Its foundation date is the principal fixed point in the known history of Greyfell Citadel, and the site’s identity remains tied to the age in which it was established.

The citadel’s present condition is **abandoned**. This status defines Greyfell Citadel more strongly than any surviving indication of its original purpose. Its former stronghold status has been reduced to abandonment, leaving the site as a grey stone or grey-hued ruin within the Shattered Vale. The description does not preserve a record of the circumstances that led to its abandonment. Accordingly, the decline of Greyfell Citadel is treated as a condition of the site rather than as an account of a specific recorded event.

Greyfell Citadel is also situated outside the realm’s core holdings. This position distinguishes it from locations incorporated into the realm’s central seat of power. The citadel’s separation from those core holdings contributes to its character as a remnant: a place retaining the visual and symbolic weight of a fortress while no longer functioning as an inhabited center of authority.

The passage of time has not erased the site’s designation. Greyfell Citadel continues to be identified by its founding date, its location in the Shattered Vale, and its abandoned state. The combination of these facts gives the citadel a narrow but enduring historical profile. It is neither described as a newly established outpost nor as an active seat whose purpose has been restored. It is a founded stronghold whose present identity is that of a deserted ruin.

The condition of Greyfell Citadel should not be confused with the absence of all affiliation. The citadel is ruled by [[House Morvain]], and [[Brannoc Palefroth]] serves at the site. These records preserve a connection between the abandoned location and continuing named associations, even though Greyfell Citadel itself remains abandoned.

## Relationships & Affiliations

Greyfell Citadel is ruled by [[House Morvain]]. This is the citadel’s stated ruling affiliation. The relationship places Greyfell Citadel within the sphere of House Morvain while leaving its physical and functional condition unchanged: the site remains abandoned and outside the realm’s core holdings.

The ruling association is significant because Greyfell Citadel is not presented as an unaffiliated ruin. Its grey stone and desolate condition do not remove it from the records of authority. Instead, the site retains a recognized connection to House Morvain despite the reduction of its former stronghold status to abandonment. No further administrative details are established in the surviving record.

[[Brannoc Palefroth]] serves at Greyfell Citadel. This service is the other named association directly attached to the location. The record identifies the connection without specifying the nature, duration, or duties of the service. As a result, Brannoc Palefroth’s presence is part of Greyfell Citadel’s documented identity, but it does not alter the site’s official status as abandoned.

The two affiliations should be understood separately. [[House Morvain]] is identified as the power by which Greyfell Citadel is ruled, while [[Brannoc Palefroth]] is identified as serving at the citadel. Neither statement supplies additional information about the site’s internal condition, its former garrison, or the extent of any current activity. Together, however, they establish that Greyfell Citadel remains connected to named authority and service even in its abandoned state.

Its placement in [[the Shattered Vale]] further defines these relationships. The region is not merely a geographic designation but the setting through which the citadel’s grey and desolate identity is understood. Greyfell Citadel is therefore recorded as a location of House Morvain’s rule and Brannoc Palefroth’s service within the Shattered Vale, while remaining outside the realm’s core holdings.

## Appearance and Atmosphere

Greyfell Citadel is characterized visually as a citadel of grey stone or a grey-hued ruin set within [[the Shattered Vale]]. The distinction between standing grey stone and grey-hued ruin is part of the site’s presentation: either form communicates age, severity, and diminished purpose. Greyfell Citadel is not defined by bright ornament or signs of renewal. Its appearance instead emphasizes the persistence of old material after the authority associated with a stronghold has faded.

The citadel’s atmosphere is one of old desolation. This quality is not simply a description of weathered surfaces; it expresses the relationship between the site’s appearance and its status. Grey stone suggests endurance, while abandonment suggests the loss of the activity that once gave the stronghold meaning. The resulting image is of a place that remains recognizable as a citadel but no longer presents itself as a living center of power.

The Shattered Vale provides the setting for this visual identity. Against the region’s ruined character, the grey of Greyfell Citadel reinforces the impression of a site shaped by age and withdrawal. The citadel is consequently read less as an active fortress than as a remnant of one. Its form preserves the memory of strength without claiming that strength as a present condition.

This atmosphere also explains why Greyfell Citadel is commonly treated as a location of old desolation. The term reflects the site’s combination of origin and present state: it was founded in **62 AS**, possessed former stronghold status, and is now abandoned. The citadel’s significance lies in that contrast. It remains substantial enough to be recognized, but diminished enough to embody the erosion of the purpose for which such a place would ordinarily exist.

Greyfell Citadel’s appearance should therefore be understood as part of its historical identity rather than as a separate decorative feature. The grey stone or grey-hued ruin, the setting in the Shattered Vale, and the condition of abandonment all reinforce one another. Its continuing rule by [[House Morvain]] and the service of [[Brannoc Palefroth]] provide documented affiliations, but the dominant impression of the site remains desolation: a former stronghold preserved as a grey and abandoned presence outside the realm’s core holdings.
