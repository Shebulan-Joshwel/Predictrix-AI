# Fenthrone

Fenthrone is a fortified coastal seat on the Pale Coast, founded in 229 AS and maintained as a garrisoned holding under the rule of the Bleeding Crown. Its visual identity is defined by the stark pallor of the surrounding coast, while its permanent military presence gives the location a watchful and disciplined character. Fenthrone remains outside the realm’s core holdings and is garrisoned by 5805 troops. Halvard Cindervale and Voltaire Hollowmere both serve at Fenthrone.
